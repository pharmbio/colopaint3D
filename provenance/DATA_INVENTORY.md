## Not included here

The per-slice CellProfiler feature dumps (`FeaturesImages_*`, ~19.5 GB) live under
`data/features/` and feed `1_Data`/`2_Processing`, not the figures — the figures run from
the tables above. Raw images are to be deposited separately in the BioImage Archive, and
the CellProfiler `.cppipe` pipelines and Cellpose models are in no repo at all
(see `provenance/KNOWN_ISSUES.md`).
